# Ireland founding-contributor outreach drafts

Status: founder approval required; do not send  
Audience: P0 founder network and the first six P1 organisations  
Related queue: `IRELAND_CONTRIBUTOR_TARGETS.md`  
System of record after sending: `IRELAND_CONTRIBUTOR_OUTREACH.csv`

## Rules for using these drafts

- Send from a named founder account, one organisation at a time.
- Use the organisation's general public channel; do not copy a published list
  of individual coaches, captains or member contact details.
- Ask for an introduction to a person. Do not ask for a route library, GPX
  bundle, member list, club Strava data or access to a private RWGPS space.
- Do not attach files or invite files by reply. The protected LOOPS workflow is
  the only intake path after staging rehearsal and contributor approval.
- Do not promise payment, membership, publicity, revenue share or route volume.
  If compensation is raised, record the question and say the founding model is
  still being decided.
- Replace every bracketed field and read the message aloud before sending.
- Do not send any P1 message until the P0 staging rehearsal passes.

## P0 — Roadman member invitation

Suggested channel: a founder-controlled Roadman member channel, sent only to
adults. This is a request for volunteers, not an export of the membership list.

**Subject:** Help us prove the new LOOPS route standard in Ireland

> I am restarting LOOPS as a reviewed library of road loops, beginning with
> Dublin and Wicklow. The rule is simple: LOOPS will never create, copy or
> publish a route unless a named person rode that exact version, controls the
> recording and a different person has reviewed it.
>
> Before we invite outside clubs, I need a small Roadman rehearsal group: two
> riders who know dependable endurance loops and two who regularly complete
> structured road sessions. The work is not to share your Strava profile or a
> folder of routes. You would apply personally, upload one of your own completed
> GPX/FIT/TCX recordings through the protected test product, check the public
> start and finish for privacy, and work through one review or revision.
>
> For workout routes, we will discuss the exact stretch, direction, junctions,
> traffic, entry, recovery and run-out. We will not label a loop from an
> elevation chart.
>
> If you are open to a 20-minute qualification call and one end-to-end test,
> reply with the broad area you ride most often. Please do not send a ride file,
> activity link or home start by message.

## Reply triage

### Positive introduction

1. Thank the organisation and ask it to let the nominee contact LOOPS directly,
   or request explicit permission before writing to the nominee.
2. Record only the organisation-level status and nominee count in the repository
   tracker. Keep the person's contact details in the approved operational system,
   not Git.
3. Schedule the qualification call. Do not request a file on the call.
4. Send the `/beta` application link only after staging is isolated and the P0
   end-to-end rehearsal passes.

### “Can we just send our files or route links?”

> Thank you, but please do not send them. A club or operator route file does not
> establish who rode it or who can grant publication rights. We need the named
> adult rider to apply and upload their own completed recording through LOOPS.
> I am happy to explain the process before you decide whether to introduce
> someone.

### Rights, insurance or privacy concern

Do not improvise an answer or minimise the concern. Record it, pause that
relationship and route it into the legal/insurance review. The public beta does
not open until the approved terms and answer are documented.

### Compensation question

> We have not finalised the founding-contributor model, so I do not want to make
> a promise in this conversation. The options under review include recognition,
> founding membership and a fixed approved-route honorarium. Evidence and review
> standards will be the same under any model. I will come back with a written
> proposal before asking for substantive work.

### Commercial partnership interest

Keep it separate from route approval. A sponsor, shop or operator cannot buy
publication or review status. Record the commercial interest for later and
continue contributor intake only through the named-rider process.

### Decline or no response

Thank a clear decline and close the tracker row. Follow up once after five
working days when there is no response, then close the attempt. Do not move to a
second competitor in the same local community merely to hit an outreach count;
use the route-coverage gaps to choose the next P2 target.
