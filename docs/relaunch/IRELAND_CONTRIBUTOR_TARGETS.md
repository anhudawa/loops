# Ireland founding-contributor target list

Status: researched, not contacted  
Research date: 25 August 2026  
Initial geography: Dublin and Wicklow  
Acquisition goal: 12–16 named people and 35 independently reviewed road loops

## What this list is for

This is a founder outreach queue, not a permission to import an organisation's
route library. Each target should be asked to nominate one or two adult riders
who personally know useful roads and are willing to apply to LOOPS in their own
name. Every eventual contribution must still satisfy the product controls:

- the contributor rode the exact route version and uploads their own completed,
  timestamped GPX, FIT or TCX recording through LOOPS;
- the contributor grants the required publication rights and can be named with
  the date or month last ridden;
- a separate curator checks the evidence, privacy, road suitability and route
  description before publication;
- workout suitability is recorded only for a specific stretch and direction
  assessed by a rider, then approved by a different reviewer;
- club Ride with GPS spaces, Strava clubs, public links, archives, member lists
  and route packs are never scraped, copied or requested;
- ride files, precise home starts and private activity data are never sent by
  email.

The target is one accountable relationship per organisation. A club or business
does not become the route's contributor unless an authorised representative
actually rode the submitted version and can make the full personal attestation.

## Selection logic

The list deliberately combines four kinds of supply:

1. Active club ride leaders give LOOPS repeat knowledge of roads, changing
   conditions and practical start points.
2. North-, south- and west-Dublin clubs reduce the risk of launching a catalogue
   made entirely of near-identical Wicklow routes.
3. Coaches provide the evidence needed to distinguish endurance, tempo, sweet
   spot and threshold terrain. They are not asked to classify a whole loop from
   an elevation chart.
4. Shops, guides and event organisers can introduce named local riders, but
   cannot hand over customer data or a pre-existing route library.

## Outreach sequence and stop/go rules

### Step 1 — rehearse before external outreach

Run the complete P0 process with at least two Roadman contributors and two
independent curators on staging: application, approval, upload, automated
checks, review, publication, route match, GPX download, ride confirmation,
condition report, quarantine and revision. Do not use production as staging.

### Step 2 — send only the P1 asks

After the rehearsal passes, contact the six P1 organisations one at a time.
The message asks for an introduction to a named person, not permission to take
routes. Log every approach in `IRELAND_CONTRIBUTOR_OUTREACH.csv`; do not put a
person's private details in the repository.

### Step 3 — measure the shape of supply

Move a target into P2 outreach only when at least one P1 contributor has
completed a valid submission. Prioritise P2 targets according to empty cells in
`IRELAND_ROUTE_SUPPLY_TRACKER.csv`, not by brand prestige or enthusiasm.

Pause outreach if any target misunderstands the ask as a bulk transfer, if
curation exceeds the five-working-day service level, or if a club/member privacy
concern cannot be answered from the approved consent and rights wording.

## First-call script

1. LOOPS is a reviewed library of road loops, not an automatic route generator.
2. We are asking for an introduction to one or two adults who know the roads
   through repeated riding; we do not want the organisation's files or member
   data.
3. Each person controls their own application and uploads only their own
   completed recording after agreeing to the publication terms.
4. LOOPS names the contributor, shows when the route was last ridden and uses a
   separate curator before anything is visible.
5. Workout suitability is never inferred from the map. A rider must identify
   the exact stretch, direction and real-world conditions, and a different
   reviewer must approve it.
6. Ask whether the organisation is comfortable making an introduction. Do not
   negotiate route ownership or compensation on the first call.

## Success criteria for this queue

- at least 12 qualified people consent and apply personally;
- no single contributor accounts for more than 20% of the public catalogue;
- at least three independent contributor relationships cover north Dublin;
- at least three cover Tallaght, Blessington or west-Wicklow access;
- at least three qualified riders complete segment-level workout assessments;
- the route catalogue reaches the 35-route target without missing the fixed
  discovery and workout coverage gates;
- zero route files, private activity links or member lists enter through email.

## Research caveat

The sources above establish that each organisation is currently active and is a
plausible source of introductions. They do not establish that any named person
has agreed to contribute, owns particular recordings or endorses LOOPS. Every
row remains `not_contacted` until the founder authorises outreach and the
corresponding communication is actually sent.
