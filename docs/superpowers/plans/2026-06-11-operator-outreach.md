## Purpose
This is the B2B lever from the pricing proposal: cycling-tourism operators (rental
shops, cycling hotels, camps, tour companies) pay €100–300/month for a branded
guest route hub — their logo, their recommended loops, plus the LOOPS generator
for their guests. The consumer product stays the priority; this is a low-effort
side channel. The plan is one email per destination (10 emails total), sent
personally by Anthony. Every business below was verified against its own live
website in June 2026; emails are only listed where they were actually published.

## Pitch email template

---
Subject: Free route hub for your guests — built by a fellow cycling business

Hi [name],

I'm Anthony Walsh — I run Roadman Cycling in Dublin and I've just built LOOPS
(loops.ie), a route-intelligence app for serious cyclists: curated classic loops
for [destination] plus an AI generator that builds wind-aware routes from a
sentence ("90km road loop from [their town], tailwind home, café at halfway").

I'd like to set up a branded route hub for your guests — your logo, your
recommended loops, GPX and head-unit sync included, free for them to use. For
you it's one less thing to explain at every bike handover; for me it's feedback
from riders who know [destination]'s roads.

I'm offering this to a handful of operators per destination. Can I show you a
10-minute demo this week?

Anthony
Roadman Cycling · loops.ie
---

## How to run this
1. Send 10 emails — one operator per destination first (pick the top row), so
   no two competitors in the same town get the same week-one offer.
2. Track in a simple sheet: business, date sent, reply, demo booked, outcome.
   Follow up once after 5 days; then move to the next operator on the list.
3. Demo = the live destination page on loops.ie + a live generator run from
   their shop's town. Ten minutes, screen share or in person.
4. No price talk before the demo. After it, anchor to the pricing proposal:
   €100–300/month depending on size. Any operator who says yes during beta
   gets founding pricing locked in.
