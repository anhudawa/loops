# LOOPS — Pricing Proposal (CEO decision memo)

**Date:** 2026-06-11 · **From:** COO · **Decision needed from:** Anthony
**Why now:** the standing order is "work non-stop until this generates
revenue." Revenue needs a price. Setting the price is your call, not the
team's — this memo gives you one clear recommendation and what it costs
to say yes.

## What the market charges (the anchor)

- Komoot Premium: **€59.99/yr** — and they just put device sync behind it.
  That decision is why riders are leaving.
- Strava subscription: **~€74.99/yr**.
- RideWithGPS: **~$60/yr**.

So serious cyclists already pay €60–75 a year for route tools. We don't
need to invent willingness to pay — we need to deserve a slice of it.

## The one rule we never break

Everything Komoot paywalled, we keep **free forever**: GPX import and
export, bulk import, device sync, your saved routes. That promise is our
entire acquisition story (the /switch page says it in writing). We charge
for intelligence, never for access to your own routes.

## Recommendation: two steps

### Step 1 — now, during beta: "Founding Rider" (first revenue)
Everything stays free for everyone while we gather real usage evidence.
Add ONE paid thing: **Founding Rider — €49 one-off**. It buys Pro for
life at the founding price, a founding badge, and a direct line to shape
the product. Cap it (e.g. first 200). This gets the first euros in the
door **without** gating any feature during the period where we need
users and retention data more than money — and every purchase is the
strongest evidence slide we can show: strangers paid before launch.

### Step 2 — at public launch: "LOOPS Pro" — **€39/yr** (or €4.99/mo)
Deliberately under Komoot's €59.99. Pro is the **training intelligence**:

| Free forever | Pro (€39/yr) |
|---|---|
| Import/export, bulk import, device sync | Unlimited AI route generation |
| All saved routes, draw planner | Session-aware workout loops (2×20 on roads that hold it) |
| Browse all destinations | Wind-planned rides ("tailwind home", best start time) |
| 3 AI generations per week | Climb alerts pushed to your head unit |

The free tier is genuinely good (better than Komoot's free tier is now);
the paid tier is the thing nobody else sells at any price.

## Secondary lever (parallel, not instead)

**LOOPS for operators (B2B):** the camps, hotels and rental fleets in
our 10 destinations pay for a branded route library + the generator for
their guests. The route-sourcing work already built these relationships'
raw material. A handful of €100–300/mo operator accounts could match
early consumer revenue. Worth one outreach email per destination once
the consumer side is live. No build needed beyond what exists.

## What saying yes costs you (owner actions)

1. **A Stripe account** (~15 minutes, free to open) — we cannot take a
   payment without it. The team can wire up checkout the same day you
   share the keys.
2. A yes/no on **€49 Founding Rider now** and **€39/yr Pro at launch**
   (or tell us different numbers — the build is the same either way).

## What the team builds once you decide

- /pricing page with the free-forever promise in writing + the table above.
- Founding Rider checkout (Stripe) + badge on profile.
- Generation metering for the free tier (only switches on at public
  launch, not during beta).

Nothing above starts until you pick the numbers. Everything else this
session (refugee funnel, usage measurement) is already being built and
doesn't wait on this.
